## July 1944 (cover img 956)
- img957: 1-2 Jul Barham, Kent (MR650682). 3 Jul Tilbury: marshalling, embarking.
- img958: 4-5 Jul Tilbury, loading; RHQ on SS Fort Assiniboine.
- img959: 6-8 Jul at sea; 8 Jul off Juno, near Courseulles.
- img960: 8 Jul RHQ landed "M" beach at Graye-sur-Mer, to marshalling, conc area near RYES 0400 hrs 9 Jul. 23 Bty: first vehs off LCTs 1045 hrs 8 Jul, last 2200, landed 2230, proceeded to Ryes. 9 Jul: all btys conc Ryes MR848829; 23 Bty vehs camouflaged in regt area at Ryes.
- img961: 10 Jul RHQ to Carpiquet MR974692. 23 Bty: recce for bty posn around ROTS (9571), moved 2245 to conc area at Bretteville (9272) [Bretteville-l'Orgueilleuse].
- img962: 11 Jul RHQ La Villeneuve MR942712. 23 Bty moved 1000 hrs to area ROTS, tps take up posns. 12 Jul 23 Bty moved 1000 to new conc area 944709, tps to Carpiquet area, posn 2330 hrs; mortar and 88mm fire.
- img963: 13 Jul 23 Bty F and G tps tied in guns. 14 Jul RHQ La Villeneuve.
- img964: 14 Jul 23 Bty no movement, all tps dug in; shelling and mortar; I and J tps posns night 14/15 Jul.
- img965: 15 Jul 23 Bty MR944709 quiet; CO visited gun posns. 16 Jul RHQ La Villeneuve; 23 Bty MR944709 slight enemy activity.
- img966: 17 Jul 23 Bty BHQ "F" same location; air raid on airfield (Carpiquet). 2 Div to attack over Orne, high ground by Basse, Ifs, St Andre sur Orne.
- img967: 18 Jul 23 Bty ready to move at short notice (Goodwood bombing S of Caen). 19 Jul: 23 Bty moving at 1800 hrs to MR030679; I and J tps move with FMR, K Tp with SSR.
- img968: 19 Jul 23 Bty: bde crossed SP 1750, move in Caen 2230, BHQ in park at 032684 [Caen]. I tp advanced with Camerons (C of C); FMR and SSR following with J and K tps; concentrate MR006678 just N of Venoix, suburb of Caen. 20 Jul: 23 Bty tps tied up rear of FUP for attack 1500 hrs, SSR centre, FMR left; Camerons advanced St Andre sur Orne. I tp near mill sq 0262. SSR in centre badly cut up. K tp lost 4 guns and towers, 7 men killed, some missing; rest filtered back to BHQ with Lt Paul. Also: "Lieut. Paul's K tp out of action". 108 Bty moved to Ifs MR036655.
- img969: 21 Jul 23 Bty engaged in tank battle N of St Andre sur Orne, one tank knocked out; I tp pinned down; Bdr Grassick (23 Bty) single-handed fired 17pr, knocked out tank. Sgt Irvine, Airries, Selsvold evac; L/Bdr Ponting and Stewart GE killed (buried MR042626 and 046624).
- img970: 22 Jul 23 Bty: Lt Paul arrived at F ech with 12 men from K tp to reinforce I and J tps; Paul went out to J tp. Usual shelling. 23 Jul RHQ Caen MR016686.
- img971: 23 Jul 23 Bty: Capt HPM Smith wounded by mortar; Capt WH Falls replaces; two remaining I tp guns withdrawn from Calgarys area, sited in C of C (Camerons) area 2330 hrs. 24 Jul RHQ moved to MR020692 [Caen]. 23 Bty: J tp att to RHC for big attack on Bacquencourt [Basse/?]. I tp withdrawn from Courevous? to Fleury-sur-Orne. BHQ moved from Ifs to Fleury-sur-Orne.
- img972: 25 Jul RHQ MR024646 [Fleury/Ifs area]. Attack by 2 and 3 Divs toward Bretteville-sur-Laize (Spring). 23 Bty: I troop moved up to Verrieres in support of RHLI night 25/26 Jul; J tp remained with RHC. 18 reinforcements for K tp but used to replace casualties in 108 Bty and J tp.
- img973: 26 Jul RHQ MR024646; 23 Bty conditions fairly quiet. RHQ to MR027658 1900 hrs.
- img974: 27 Jul 23 Bty: BC, LO and CO recce of new bde area; I and J tps withdrawn to F and A ech areas 2300 for rest. 108 Bty crews N of St Andre relieved by 23 Bty.
- img975: 28 Jul 23 Bty: BC and LO visited 6 Bde comd at new location at BASSE, preparatory to I and J tps taking over gun posns in ST ANDRE SUR ORNE; I and J tps moved up 2315, accomplished.
- img976: 29 Jul RHQ MR027658. 23 Bty: 17pr tp of 20 Bty under 23 Bty comd to defend FMR area; LO took tp comd; I and J tps heavy mortaring on posns. 
- img977: 30 Jul 23 Bty: seven K tp men reported missing in action 20 Jul confirmed in hospitals and Fd Amb. 31 Jul.
- img978: July appendices list (incl. 11 Reports on Tank Engagements).
## August 1944 (cover img1125; sheets from 1128)
- img1128: 1 Aug RHQ MR027658 Fleury-sur-Orne. 23 Bty: BC out for SABOT trials; Lt Paul to 17pr school. 2 Aug: 23 Bty no change in location.
- img1130: 2 Aug 23 Bty tps quiet night in orchard; Robot tanks? untrained personnel to 17pr school. 3 Aug 23 Bty: "I" Tp confirmed knocking out one Panther; mortaring on tps.
- img1131: 4 Aug 23 Bty: 5 Bde replacing 6 Bde at St Andre sur Orne; 23 Bty now in support of 5 Bde. RHQ sheet 40/16 SW.
- img1132: 5 Aug 23 Bty: BC liaising with Black Watch re gun dispositions; I and J tps preparing to move forward.
- img1133: 6 Aug 23 Bty: J tp in posn at St Martin-de-Fontenay 022605; shelling and mortaring; bty in support of 4 Bde. 7 Aug (Totalize): 23 Bty J tp and E tp (20 Bty) with Camerons (C of C), I tp with FMR; Gnr Forrester RR, I tp, killed by mortar fire. 2200 hrs bty concentrated just N of Troteval Fm.
- img1134: 8 Aug 23 Bty: J tp taking up posns N of Fontenay-le-Marmion on the road: 2 quads and 1 gun knocked out by 88mm; skirmish, 6 prisoners. I tp in posn in orchard N of St Andre sur Orne. K tp in support of 20 Bty. (6 CIB mopping up Rocquancourt, Fontenay-le-Marmion, May-sur-Orne.)
- img1135: 9 Aug btys deployed N of Bretteville-sur-Laize, Fontenay-le-Marmion, May-sur-Orne, Rocquancourt. 23 Bty: Lt Anderson took comd I tp. 10 Aug RHQ May-sur-Orne MR020591; 23 Bty A ech to MR019588, B ech MR005628.
- img1136: 11 Aug RHQ May-sur-Orne; 23 Bty came out of action for rest, concentrated MR034588.
- img1137: 12 Aug RHQ MR033548 "Aux-Gaugins" sheet 40/14NW. 23 Bty in concentration with one tp of 6prs deployed in area Fontenay-le-Marmion; bty in rest area, maintenance; A ech to MR034548.
- img1138: 13 Aug RHQ MR033548; 23 Bty in reserve at Fontenay-le-Marmion (still in rest area, gun drill and maint).
- img1139: 14 Aug 23 Bty: I and K tp in rest area; J tp deployed with 6 Fd Regt; isolated pockets of enemy on left flank. 15 Aug: advance nearing Falaise.
- img1140: 15 Aug 23 Bty I and K in rest area, J tp with 6 Fd Regt. 16 Aug RHQ "Aunay sur Odon" MR071456 (sic; grid is near Potigny). 23 Bty moved to area 069463, guns deployed for protection of Div HQ; BHQ MR087457; I tp 069473, J tp 074459, K tp 081467 (Potigny itself is MR110448). 17 Aug: 20, 23 and 108 btys deployed around southern perimeter of FALAISE.
- img1141: 17 Aug 23 Bty BHQ moved MR123383, tps deployed around FALAISE: I tp with FMR 147348, J tp with Camerons 146335, K tp with SSR 151355; A ech 121385; J tp bombed and machine-gunned by own fighters, four wounded. 18 Aug RHQ Falaise MR119392.
- img1142: 18 Aug 23 Bty guns in posn. 19 Aug 23 Bty going fwd to deploy in new 5 Bde area; moved to area 257358: I tp with FMR 231373, J tp with Camerons 261350, K tp with SSR 240355; A ech MR203399. (18 Bty at Norrey-en-Auge 277378 next day.)
- img1143: 20 Aug RHQ St Pierre sur Dives MR211404 (sheet 7F/4). 23 Bty quiet day, guns deployed along the north side of the Falaise gap. 21 Aug: 18, 20, 23 btys A echs moved to Bde A ech areas; regt moves across SP 2000 hrs.
- img1144: 21 Aug 23 Bty BHQ early move with Bde to area 288409; tps moved with bns, then to conc area 323414. 22 Aug RHQ MR419681 (sheet 7F/4). 23 Bty: I and K tps reported to FMR and Camerons at first light; guns did not deploy; BHQ moved with 6 Bde to area 531718; I and K tps still with FMR and Camerons. 108 Bty: deployed just W of river La Touques, W of Livarot.
- img1145: 23 Aug RHQ Orbec MR570705 (sheet 8F/3). 23 Bty: I and K tps to FMR and Camerons at first light, guns did not deploy. 24 Aug RHQ Drucourt MR710817. 23 Bty tps moving with F ech of bns: I tp FMR, J tp SSR, K tp Camerons; BHQ left area 631718 1615 hrs, arrived new area 815875 at 2345 hrs. Bde HQ moved to MR686856 just W of Thiberville.
- img1146: 25 Aug RHQ Brionne MR840905 (sheet 8F/2). 23 Bty BHQ moved to MR861885; I tp and K tp each captured one prisoner. 26 Aug RHQ Brionne MR925941; 23 Bty with 6 CIB; BHQ moved across river RISLE with 6 CIB to area 959956 at 2200 hrs.
- img1147/1148 (same sheet twice): 27 Aug RHQ MR025991 (Bourgtheroulde area, sheet 8F/2). 20 Bty with 4 CIB, 23 Bty with 5 CIB, 108 Bty with 6 CIB (sic in this line). 23 Bty: O Gp 0600 at 6 Bde; bns moved fwd 0830 to new posn 035019; "I" tp captured 9 prisoners; "J" tp only tp with guns deployed. 108 Bty just W of Elbeuf; 4 Bde resistance in Foret de la Londe.
- img1149: 28 Aug 23 Bty: Major brought Cdn airmen (shot down 3 months ago, living with French) to Bde HQ; shelling in J tp area; Lt McCullough took over K tp. 29 Aug 23 Bty with 6 CIB.
- img1150: 29 Aug 23 Bty: Lts Shaughnessy and McCullough with Tac HQ until noon, then returned with tps to BHQ area; I tp moved up to new posn 2000 hrs covering cross rds at 066051 (Brionne sheet). 30 Aug 23 Bty maintenance, still same area; I tp with FMR. 31 Aug RHQ Rouen MR231076 (sheet 9E/3); 18 Bty crossed Seine.
- img1151: 31 Aug 23 Bty moved from area 035019 to area 170108 at 1400 hrs; three prisoners taken by Bty HQ. Bde ordered to advance to Totes (181413). 108 Bty crossed Seine, conc S of Rouen.
## September 1944 (cover img1257)
- img1260: 1 Sep RHQ Neufchatel MR236474 then "St Valery en Caux" MR163632 (sheet 8D/6, harbour area near Dieppe). Maj Lennon, OC 23 Bty, killed in morning (later: died in Rouen Gen Hosp). 23 Bty: 0200 left area 170108 (Rouen) with 6 Bde; I tp with FMR to 177177; left 177177 at 1430 with 6 Bde; K tp with bty, J tp with Camerons, I tp with FMR; moved to area 243633 near Dieppe. 2 Sep: 23 Bty moved 0930 to regt conc area MR160633; maint for 2 Div march past in Dieppe Sunday.
- img1261: 3 Sep 23 Bty RC church parade at OFFRANVILLE 0930; memorial service for men who fell in battle 1030; regt'l convoy to parade in Dieppe 1430 (Army Comd Crerar took salute). 4 Sep 23 Bty resting, maint, pay parade.
- img1262: 5 Sep 23 Bty maint, prepared to move. 6 Sep RHQ Montreuil MR772308 (sheet 60?). 23 Bty 0500 left in convoy with RHQ; LO recce area at 030598(?); bty and RHQ arrived 0400 in downpour.
- img1263: 7 Sep RHQ St Omer MR028567: RHQ and 23 Bty arrived in area MR028564; 23 Bty slept till 1000, maint and drying clothes. 8 Sep St Omer; 23 Bty: Lt McCullough on recce of new area MR451795 at 1330; bty prepared to move. 18 Bty ordered to Dunkerque area.
- img1264: 9 Sep RHQ "Poperinghe" MR443703 sheet 40: regt crossed Belgian border 0820, conc area. 23 Bty left 0300 hrs, arrived 0800 hrs at MR451795 (Isenberghe/Izenberge area, Belgium; 18 Bty at Isenberghe 440792). 10 Sep: btys to deploy covering enemy escape routes from Dunkerque and coastal strip E of Dunkerque.
- img1265: 10 Sep 23 Bty bath parade; sports; film "Song of Russia" in town hall at ISENBERGHE. 11 Sep Tac HQ MR285738; 18 Bty S of Bergues, 20 Bty S of Dunkerque.
- img1266: 11 Sep 23 Bty maint for CO's inspection; film in town hall Isenberghe. 12 Sep: two 23 Bty 17prs into posn area MR330970 by first light to engage enemy pillboxes; 23 Bty assisted in making a concrete emplacement fall and 316 prisoners taken; 17pr AP vs walls 15 ft thick; destroyed church steeple (possible enemy OP). K tp went forward 0330 with 2 guns to knock out pillbox at MR325918, opened fire 1400 hrs with ack-ack support.
- img1267: 12 Sep (cont) Germans surrendered and 300 prisoners incl 8 offrs gave themselves up to the Essex Scottish. 13 Sep btys engaging targets in area BERGUES and DUNKERQUE; 23 Bty maint, training, film "This Happy Breed" in town hall at Isenberghe. 14 Sep RHQ and bty A echs moved to MR270672.
- img1268: 14 Sep 23 Bty: Lt McCullough recce new area 1100; bty moved with RHQ to new area MR262677 sheet 40 at 1645 hrs. 15 Sep RHQ "Poperinghe" MR270672: regt supporting inf bdes surrounding Dunkerque; 4 CIB to move to area near Antwerp.
- img1269: 15 Sep 23 Bty fine day, maint and gun drill, sports. 16 Sep 18 Bty moved to Antwerp with 4 Bde (route Poperinghe-Ypres-Roulers-Thielt-Ghent-Termonde). 23 Bty maint and gun drill; 9 OR reinforcements: 5 to K troop, 3 to J troop, 1 to BHQ.
- img1270: 17 Sep RHQ, 23 Bty and LAD moved to new location at MR734904 sheet 24&34, "BRECHT-LIERRE" ("this location is near ANTWERP"). Dakotas going toward Holland; paratroop invasion of Holland reported (Market Garden).
- img1271: 17 Sep 23 Bty: Lt McCullough recce 0615; bty left in convoy with RHQ 0615, arrived new area 735905 (sheet 24&34) at 1530 hrs. 18 Sep RHQ Brecht-Lierre MR734904: out of comms with all sub-units except 23 Bty; 20 Bty with 5 CIB to Antwerp area.
- img1272: 18 Sep 23 Bty gun drill and maint, route march. 19 Sep RHQ MR734904: 20, 23 and 108 btys now concentrated; 23 Bty gun drill, lecture, bath parade, DM school.
- img1273: 20 Sep 23 Bty pay parade, gun drill, school; Lts Anderson and Tompkins on loan to 18 Bty. 108 Bty concentrated at MR715906 at MORTSEL just south of Antwerp; "RHQ which is only about 500 yds away" => RHQ/23 Bty at Mortsel area. 21 Sep.
- img1274: 21 Sep 23 Bty DM school, gun drill, maint, route march. 22 Sep 23 Bty: one gun from J tp attached to engineers for loading drill; offrs regt'l dance at RHQ (guests from Antwerp). 23 Sep.
- img1275: 23 Sep 23 Bty: J tp gun returned; gun drill, maint, sports. 24 Sep 23 Bty RC church parade 0930, Protestant parade at RHQ 1100. 25 Sep.
- img1276: 25 Sep 23 Bty maint, gun drill, route march; Major RJ Hamilton took comd of the Bty. 26 Sep 23 Bty gun drill, route march, bath parade; one 6pr and jeep to engineers for bridging trials.
- img1277: 27 Sep 23 Bty gun drill; T16 and 17pr to engineers. 28 Sep RHQ MR796983: RHQ, 23 Bty, 18 and 108 A echs moved to area MR796983 (approx 8 miles E of Antwerp; 18 Bty: "A ech moved approx 8 miles east of Antwerp along with RHQ"). 23 Bty moved 1000 hrs with RHQ to new area MR796982 sht 34 NW.
- img1278: 29 Sep 5 CIB and 20 Bty across Antwerp-Turnhout Canal. 23 Bty took over from 108 Bty with 6 CIB in afternoon; I, J and K tps joined SSR, Camerons, FMR at 1400 hrs; BHQ moved to area 876945. 30 Sep 23 Bty: I and J tps changed bns, I to FMR, J to SSR.
